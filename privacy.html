Privacy Policy

ClipAgent · Last updated 19 November 2026

Summary

ClipAgent runs only on its owner's computer. It has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

What it stores

  • Platform sign-in tokens (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram): stored locally in Windows Credential Manager on the owner's computer, and used only to upload videos to the owner's own account.
  • Basic account info from TikTok (display name, from the user.info.basic scope): shown locally so the owner can see which account is connected.
  • Clips and stream data: video clips, transcripts and public Twitch chat messages used to find moments, all stored locally and deleted automatically after a set time.

What it sends

Videos, titles and captions are sent only to the platform the owner chose to post to, through that platform's official API. Nothing is sent to any other third party.

Deleting data

Disconnecting an account in ClipAgent deletes its stored token. Access can also be revoked from the platform's own settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions). Deleting ClipAgent's data folder removes everything else.

Children

ClipAgent is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
