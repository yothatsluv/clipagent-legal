What ClipAgent is

ClipAgent is a personal, self-hosted tool. It runs on its owner's computer, creates short video clips from Twitch streams the owner has permission to use, and uploads them to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram through each platform's official API.

Who can use it

ClipAgent is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it or post through it.

Content

The owner is responsible for every clip ClipAgent posts. Clips are only made from channels the owner owns or has the creator's permission to clip and republish. All posts must follow the rules of the platform they are posted to, including the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Platform accounts

Connecting a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account lets ClipAgent upload videos to that account only. The owner can disconnect at any time from ClipAgent or from the platform's account settings.

No warranty

ClipAgent is provided as-is, without any warranty. It may stop working if a platform changes its API.
